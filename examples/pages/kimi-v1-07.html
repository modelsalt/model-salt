Good questions

Everything parents ask us

Do I need to be a good cook to use Plantry? +
Not even a little. Every recipe uses everyday ingredients, clear step-by-step instructions, and zero fancy techniques. If you can boil water and operate a sheet pan, you're overqualified.
What if my kids hate everything? +
Then you're exactly who we built Picky-Eater Mode for. Set each child's rules (no green things, sauce on the side, cheese mandatory) and every plan adapts. Our hidden-veggie recipes have a 94% "they never noticed" rate. Yes, we track this.
Can it handle allergies and dietary restrictions? +
Absolutely. Gluten-free, dairy-free, nut allergies, vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher — set it once per family member and Plantry filters every recipe and grocery item automatically. Allergens are flagged on every recipe card.
What if I don't like a suggested meal? +
Swap it in one tap — Plantry instantly offers alternatives that use similar ingredients, so your grocery list stays tight. You can also lock favorites, ban ingredients forever, or regenerate the whole week.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yep — two taps in the app, no emails, no phone calls, no guilt trips. Your saved recipes stay yours on the free plan forever. (Though 9 out of 10 families who try a planned week never go back.)
