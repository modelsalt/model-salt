Questions

Good questions.

Still wondering? Email hello@mise.app. A real person replies, usually before dinner.

Do I have to cook every night?

Not at all. Choose how many nights you want planned. Mise fills the gaps with leftovers, "fridge-clear" nights, or simply leaves them open for takeout.

Can it handle allergies and mixed diets?

Yes. Each person gets a profile. Allergies are hard rules and are never broken; preferences are soft and Mise finds dishes that work for everyone, or suggests easy split versions.

Where do the recipes come from?

Our test kitchen develops and cooks every recipe at least three times. You can also import your own from any website or a photo of grandma's recipe card.

Which grocery services do you support?

Send your list to most major delivery services in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, or just use the in-app checklist at the store.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel in two taps from settings. Your saved recipes and plans stay with you on the Free plan.
