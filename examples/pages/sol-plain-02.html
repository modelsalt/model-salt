A few things you might be wondering.

What is Sprout?

Sprout brings weekly recipes and grocery lists together. Tell us what you enjoy, choose your meals, and head into the week with a plan that feels like you.

Can I plan around my dietary preferences?

Choose plant-forward, vegetarian, high-protein, or a bit of everything. Swap recipes to suit your week, and check individual ingredients for allergies.

Do I need to be a good cook?

Just bring an appetite. Our recipes use everyday ingredients, clear steps, and realistic cooking times. Start with quick and easy options to find your rhythm.
