    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Do I need to be a good cook to use Platefull?
    Not at all. Most of our recipes are 30 minutes or less with clear step-by-step instructions. If you can follow a recipe, you can use Platefull. And if you want to plan a week of "pasta, tacos, leftovers, takeout," that's a perfectly valid week.
    Can I add my own recipes?
    Yes. Type them in, snap a photo of a recipe card, or paste a link from any recipe website and we'll import the ingredients and steps. Free accounts can add 20 recipes; Plus and Household are unlimited.
    Does it work with dietary restrictions?
    Platefull supports vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, halal, kosher and low-FODMAP filters, plus custom "never show me" ingredients. Household plans let each person set their own.
    Which grocery delivery services can I send my list to?
    Plus and Household subscribers can send lists to Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart, Kroger and Tesco, with more arriving regularly. You can also share the list as plain text to anyone.
    Is there a web version?
    Yes. Your plan syncs between the iOS app, Android app and the web app at app.platefull.com, so you can plan on a laptop and shop with your phone.
    Can I cancel anytime?
    Of course. Cancel from inside the app and you'll drop back to the Free plan at the end of your billing period. Your recipes and plans stay put.
