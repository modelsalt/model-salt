Why Plated

Everything between “what’s for dinner?” and “that was good”

One app for planning, shopping, and cooking — built to adapt to real life, not the other way around.

Plans that learn your taste

Rate meals with a tap. Plated gets smarter every week, surfacing dishes you’ll actually want to cook — and quietly dropping the ones you skip.

One-tap grocery lists

Every plan turns into a list organized by store aisle. Already have garlic? Check your pantry and it disappears. Sends to Instacart or your notes app.

Fits your actual week

Late meeting Tuesday? Soccer Thursday? Tell Plated your schedule and it slots 15-minute meals on busy nights and saves the slow braise for Sunday.

Any diet, every allergy

Vegan, keto, gluten-free, low-FODMAP, nut-free kids — set it once per household member and every suggestion respects it. No exceptions.

Budget-aware by default

Set a weekly grocery budget and Plated plans within it, pairing recipes that share ingredients so nothing wilts in the back of the fridge.

Shared with the household

Everyone sees the plan. Anyone can swap a meal, claim a cooking night, or tick off the list in the store. Fewer texts, fewer duplicate cartons of milk.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Paste a link from almost any recipe site, snap a photo of a cookbook page, or type it in. Plated extracts the ingredients and adds it to your library so it can show up in future plans and grocery lists.

What if I don’t like a meal in my plan?

Swipe it away and Plated instantly offers alternatives that still fit your week, your budget, and the ingredients you already have. The more you swap, the better it learns.

Does it work for multiple dietary restrictions in one house?

That’s one of the main reasons we built it. Each household member has their own profile — allergies, preferences, portion size — and Plated finds meals that work for everyone, or suggests easy per-person tweaks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel from the app in two taps and you’ll keep access through the end of your billing period. Your recipes and history stay in your free Starter account.

Is my data private?

Your meal and health data is yours. We never sell it, and we don’t share it with grocery partners unless you choose to export a list. You can delete your account and all data at any time.
