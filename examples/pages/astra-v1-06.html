Good questions

Before we
set the table.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Adjust the household size in the preview, then download your sample week below. This page works entirely in your browser and doesn’t ask for personal information.

What counts as a portion?

The preview counts one standard portion per person, per dinner. Appetites vary, so treat the ingredient quantities as a starting point and adjust for your household.

What if I want leftovers?

Add extra people to the sample portion count. For example, choose four portions when cooking for two if you’d like another meal for the next day.

Is this the full app?

This is an interactive preview of Tableful. It includes a fixed three-meal week, adjustable portions, and a downloadable starter plan. Recipe browsing and saved accounts aren’t part of this demo.
