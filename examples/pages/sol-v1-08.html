A few things you might wonder

Pull up a chair.

Can we plan together as a household?

That’s what Gather is made for. Share a household plan, choose meals together, and use the same shopping list so everyone knows what’s needed.

What if everyone likes different food?

Save the meals your household loves and keep preferences in mind as you build your week. Flexible meals like bowls and tacos leave room for everyone’s own finishing touches.

Do I have to plan every single meal?

Start with two or three dinners. Keep room for leftovers, a meal out, or a change of plans. A useful plan fits your life.
