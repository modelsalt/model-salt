How it works

Sunday, 5 minutes. That's the whole ritual.

No recipe hunting, no list writing, no 6pm panic.

1

Tell us about your table

How many people, what you like, what you avoid, how much time you have on weeknights. Takes about two minutes, once.

2

Get your week, tweak if you want

Forkcast drafts seven dinners (plus lunches and breakfasts if you like). Swipe to swap any meal. Lock your favorites so they stick around.

3

Shop once, cook calmly

Your grocery list is ready before you finish your coffee. Each night, open the app for step-by-step cooking mode with built-in timers.
