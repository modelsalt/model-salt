RECIPE SPEC #042 // POST-WORKOUT

Charred Atlantic Cod, Forbidden Black Rice & Pickled Kombu

High-glycemic replenishment paired with rapid-digesting marine protein and trace electrolytes.

PROTEIN (68G) 31% OF MEAL
CARBOHYDRATES (115G) 52% OF MEAL
LIPIDS (16G) 17% OF MEAL
PREP: 10 MIN COOK: 18 MIN TOTAL: 890 KCAL

SCALED INGREDIENTS (1 ATHLETE)

  • Wild Atlantic Cod Loin, skinless280g
  • Dry Forbidden Black Rice140g
  • Rehydrated Kombu & Wakame45g
  • Cold-Pressed Rapeseed Oil12ml
  • Fermented White Miso Paste18g
  • Rice Vinegar & flaky sea salt15ml

EXECUTION PROTOCOL

  • 01 Rinse black rice thoroughly. Cook at a 1:1.75 water ratio in a pressurized vessel for 18 minutes.
  • 02 Pat cod loin dry. Whisk white miso with 10ml warm water and brush evenly over the fish surface.
  • 03 Sear in carbon steel pan with rapeseed oil for 3 minutes per side until internal temperature hits 58°C.
  • 04 Fold rice vinegar and sliced kombu into steamed rice. Plate immediately to preserve resistant starch profile.
