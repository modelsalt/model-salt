FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find yours? Email hello@sunday.example and a real person will reply.

Do I have to cook every single night?

Not at all. Tell Sunday which nights you eat out, order in, or wing it, and it plans only the rest. Planned leftovers cover the gaps.

Can it handle allergies and picky eaters?

Yes. Set hard rules (allergies, diets) and soft preferences (no mushrooms for the 7-year-old). Household plans can keep separate tastes for each person.

Does it work with grocery delivery?

You can export your list to the major delivery services in one tap, or share it as plain text to any app you like.

Can I add my own recipes?

Paste a link from almost any recipe site, or type in Grandma's lasagna by hand. Sunday reads the ingredients and folds them into your list.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

You drop back to the Free plan and keep your saved recipes and history. No hoops, no hidden fees.
