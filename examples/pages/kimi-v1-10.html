QUESTIONS

Everything parents ask us.

When does Plantry launch?

We're inviting waitlist members in batches starting this summer, in waitlist order. The earlier you join, the earlier you get in — and your 50%-off-for-life price locks in the moment you sign up.

How is this different from a recipe app?

Recipe apps give you a million options and zero decisions. Plantry makes the decisions for you: a full week of dinners matched to your schedule, your kids' preferences, and your time limits — plus the merged grocery list to cook it all.

My kid eats roughly five foods. Will this actually help?

You're exactly who we built it for. Set each child's "safe foods" and hard no's once, and plans always include something they'll eat — plus one gentle "exposure" dish with zero pressure. Every recipe carries a kid-approval rating from our beta families.

What about allergies and dietary needs?

Filters cover the top 9 allergens (gluten, dairy, nuts, and more), plus vegetarian, vegan, and other patterns. Allergen filters are hard rules, not suggestions — flagged recipes never enter your plan.

Can I use my own recipes?

Absolutely. Import from a link in one tap or type in grandma's lasagna by hand. Your recipes join the rotation and get counted in your grocery list automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel in two taps, no emails, no retention maze. You'll drop to the free tier and keep your recipe box and past plans forever.
