FAQ

Quick answers
for busy people.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@forkcast.app. A real human replies within a day.

When does Forkcast launch?

We open to the public in January 2027 on iOS, Android and the web. Waitlisters get in first, in batches starting mid-December, in the order they signed up.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Early access before the public launch, three months of the Weeknight or Household plan free, and founder pricing locked in for life if you're among the first 10,000.

Can it handle my diet?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-carb, halal, kosher-style and high-protein, plus any ingredient you just can't stand. You can mix filters per household member too.

I'm a terrible cook. Will this work for me?

That's exactly who we built it for. Recipes use one or two pans, everyday ingredients, and step-by-step mode with timers built in. If you can boil water, you can do this.

Does it really read my calendar?

Only if you let it, and only busy/free blocks. We never see event titles, attendees or details. You can disconnect anytime in one tap.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. No contracts, no "call us to cancel". You can cancel from settings in two taps and keep access until the end of your billing period.
