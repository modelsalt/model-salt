Questions parents ask us

Didn't find yours? Email hello@supperly.example and a human will reply.

How is Supperly different from saving recipes on Pinterest?

Recipes are the easy part. Supperly plans the week — matching meals to your actual schedule, your kids' preferences, and your budget, then turning it into one grocery list. It's the thinking, not the recipe, that it takes off your plate.

What if my kids are extremely picky?

That's our specialty. Each family member gets their own preference profile, and the planner optimizes for meals everyone will eat. You can also set "safe sides" that appear alongside anything new.

Can I use my own family recipes?

Yes. Paste a link, snap a photo of a recipe card, or type it in. Supperly parses the ingredients and includes them in your grocery list like any other meal.

Does it handle allergies and dietary needs?

Supperly supports nut-free, gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher-style, and custom exclusions. Allergen filters are strict and applied to every suggestion.

What happens after the free trial?

Your 14-day trial includes everything. Afterward, it's $7.99/month or $59/year for the whole household. No credit card is needed to start, and we'll remind you before the trial ends.

Which grocery stores can I send my list to?

Instacart, Walmart, Kroger, Amazon Fresh, and Target in the US; Tesco and Ocado in the UK. You can also just use the in-app checklist at any store.
