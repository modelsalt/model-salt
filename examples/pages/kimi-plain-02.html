FAQ

Good questions

Do I have to follow the plan exactly?

Not at all. Swap any meal, drag dinners between days, or add your own recipes. The plan is a starting point, not a contract — Plateful adjusts your grocery list automatically when you change things.

What if my household has different diets?

Set dietary preferences per person — say, one vegetarian and one gluten-free eater — and Plateful prioritizes meals everyone can share, or suggests simple modifications (like a GF pasta swap) when it can't.

Can I import my own recipes?

Yes. Paste a link from almost any recipe site and Plateful pulls in the ingredients and steps, so your family favorites can live right alongside ours in your weekly plan.

Does the grocery list work with delivery services?

Pro and Family plans can send your list to major grocery delivery services with one tap, or you can shop in-store with the built-in check-off mode.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely — no contracts, no cancellation fees. If you cancel, you keep Pro access until the end of your billing period, and your plans and recipes stay saved on the free tier.
