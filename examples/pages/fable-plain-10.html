Questions

Things people ask before dinner

What if I hate a meal it suggests?

Tap it, swipe it away, and it’s gone — the grocery list updates in the same breath. Mise also remembers: after a couple of swaps it stops suggesting anything in that neighbourhood.

Does it handle allergies and diets?

Yes. Set hard rules per person (nut allergy, coeliac, vegetarian) and soft preferences (“less dairy”, “more fish”). Hard rules are never broken, even when you ask Mise to remix the week.

Can I use my own recipes?

Paste a link, snap a photo of a cookbook page, or type it in. Mise extracts the ingredients and steps, and your recipes get planned alongside everything else.

Which grocery stores does it work with?

Lists work anywhere — they’re sorted by aisle for whatever store you pick. Live pricing and one-tap checkout currently cover 40+ chains across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, with more arriving monthly.

What happens after the trial?

If you do nothing, you simply drop to the free Solo plan. We don’t take a card up front, so there’s nothing to forget to cancel.
