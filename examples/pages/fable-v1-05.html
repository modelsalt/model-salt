What's in the basket

Everything between "what's for dinner?" and dinner.

Larder isn't a recipe catalogue. It's the bit of the week that usually happens on the back of an envelope — done properly.

Weekly plan in one tap

Tell Larder how many nights you're cooking and who's eating. It fills the week with meals that fit your tastes, budget, and the time you actually have.

One list, sorted your way

Every ingredient rolled into a single list, grouped by supermarket aisle or by market stall. Tick things off as you go; it syncs to whoever's shopping.

Seasonal by default

Plans lean on what's cheap and good right now. Come October you'll see squash and pears, not asparagus flown in from the other side of the world.

Leftovers, used up

Log half a cabbage or yesterday's roast chicken and Larder works it into the next couple of days before it goes to waste.

Swap anything, instantly

Plans change. Swap Wednesday's curry for something quicker and the shopping list quietly rewrites itself — no double-buying, no missing onions.

Built for households

Share the plan with your partner, flatmates, or the kids. Everyone sees what's for dinner, who's cooking, and what still needs picking up.
