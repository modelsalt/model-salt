Questions

Fair things to wonder.

Do I have to cook every night?

No. Tell Mise how many nights you actually want to cook — two, four, whatever — and it fills the rest with leftovers, "assembly" meals, or leaves them blank for eating out. Batch-cook mode can also turn one Sunday session into three weeknight dinners.

What if I don't like a suggested meal?

Swap it with one tap. Mise offers alternatives that use overlapping ingredients so your grocery list stays short. Every swap also teaches it what you like, so plans get more accurate over time.

Can I add my own family recipes?

Yes. Paste a link, type it in, or photograph a recipe card. Mise extracts the ingredients, adds it to your rotation, and includes it in grocery lists like any other recipe.

Does it work for allergies and dietary restrictions?

Mise supports the 14 major allergens plus vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, kosher-style, and more — set per household member. Nutrition+ adds medically guided templates reviewed by registered dietitians.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel in two taps from settings, no email required. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your recipes and history stay available on the free plan.
