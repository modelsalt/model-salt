    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Still curious? Write to hello@mise.app. A real human replies, usually before dinner.

    Can it handle allergies and strict diets?+

    Yes. Mise supports 30+ diets and filters out allergens at the ingredient level, including hidden sources like fish sauce or whey. Each profile in your household can have its own rules.

    Do I have to cook every night?+

    Not at all. Mark leftover nights, eating-out nights or "something from the freezer" nights, and Mise scales portions and the grocery list to match.

    Which grocery stores do you work with?+

    One-tap ordering through Instacart, Walmart, Kroger and Amazon Fresh in the US, with more regions coming. You can always export a plain list to take to any store.

    What happens after the free trial?+

    Nothing scary. If you don't pick a plan, you drop to the free Taster tier and keep your saved recipes. We'll remind you three days before the trial ends.
