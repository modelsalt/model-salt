🥕 Why Plateful

Everything dinner should be. Nothing it shouldn't.

One app that replaces your recipe bookmarks, sticky notes, and the nightly 5pm panic.

Smart weekly planner

Drag, drop, done. Plateful fills your week with meals matched to your taste, schedule, and how much energy you have on a Tuesday.

Instant grocery lists

Your list writes itself — merged, measured, and sorted by aisle. Share it with your household and check things off together.

Every diet covered

Vegan, keto, gluten-free, FODMAP, allergies, picky toddlers — filter once and every suggestion respects it, automatically.

AI pantry rescue

Snap a photo of your fridge and get recipes that use what's about to expire — before it becomes expensive compost.

Built for households

Different diets under one roof? Plateful finds the overlap and scales portions per person — no more cooking two dinners.

Nutrition, minus the math

Macros, fiber, and protein tracked quietly in the background. Set goals, and your plan gently steers you toward them.
