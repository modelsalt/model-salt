FAQ

Good questions.

Do I have to cook every night?

Not at all. Choose how many nights you want to cook, and mark nights for eating out or leftovers. Simmer only plans what you need.

Can it handle allergies and dietary restrictions?

Yes. Set allergies (nuts, shellfish, gluten and more) and diets like vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, low-FODMAP or halal per person. Allergens are hard filters and never just suggestions.

Where do the recipes come from?

Our in-house test kitchen develops and tests every recipe at least three times. You can also import recipes from any website or snap a photo of a cookbook page.

Can I share plans with my partner or roommates?

Everyone on a Household plan sees the same week and the same live grocery list. Check something off in the store and it disappears from their phone too.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

You keep your saved recipes and drop back to the Free plan. No hoops, no "are you sure?" maze, and you can cancel in two taps from settings.
