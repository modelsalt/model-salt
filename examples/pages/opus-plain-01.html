      FAQ

      Good questions, answered.

      Still curious? Email hello@forkcast.app. A real person replies, usually within the day.

      Can it handle allergies and picky eaters?

      Yes. Each person in your household gets a profile with allergies, dislikes, and diets. Forkcast never suggests a meal that breaks a hard rule, and it'll suggest simple swaps (like "serve the sauce on the side") for softer preferences.

      Do I need to be a good cook?

      Not at all. Every recipe is rated for difficulty, and you can tell Forkcast to stick to beginner-friendly meals. Step-by-step cook mode keeps your screen awake and runs timers for you.

      Which grocery stores does it work with?

      Lists export to Instacart, Walmart, Kroger, Amazon Fresh, and most major U.S. and Canadian grocers. You can also just print it or share a live checklist.

      What if I don't follow the plan exactly?

      That's expected. Mark a night as "eating out" or move meals around and Forkcast rebalances leftovers and your list automatically.

      Can I cancel anytime?

      Yes. Cancel from settings in two taps. If you cancel within 30 days of a paid plan, we'll refund you, no questions asked.
