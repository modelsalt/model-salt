FAQ

Good questions.

Still curious? Email hello@simmer.app. A real person (usually Priya) writes back.

Does it work for special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-FODMAP, halal, kosher-style, high-protein and more. You can also exclude any individual ingredient, and allergies are treated as hard rules, never suggestions.

I'm not a confident cook. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Set your skill level to "keep it simple" and Simmer favors short ingredient lists, minimal prep and one-pan meals. Every recipe has step-by-step photos and built-in timers.

How does pantry scanning work?

Snap a grocery receipt, scan barcodes, or tap items from a quick checklist. Simmer tracks rough quantities and nudges you to use things before they turn.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Paste a link from almost any recipe site, or type in Grandma's lasagna by hand. Simmer reads the ingredients and folds them into your plans and lists.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel in two taps from settings, no phone calls or guilt trips. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your recipes stay saved on the free plan.
