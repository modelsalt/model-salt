FAQ

Questions people ask before dinner.

Didn’t find yours? Email hello@forkcast.app — a human replies.

Do I have to cook every night?

No. Tell Forkcast how many nights you actually want to cook (even if that’s two) and it fills the rest with leftovers, quick assemblies, or “eat out” placeholders. We plan for the week you’ll really have.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Paste a link, snap a photo of a cookbook page, or type it in. Your recipes get the same ingredient-sharing and grocery-list treatment as ours.

How does the fridge scan work?

Take a photo of your fridge or pantry shelf, or scan a receipt. Forkcast identifies the items, estimates how long they’ll last, and prioritizes recipes that use them before they go bad. You can always edit the list by hand.

What if someone in my house eats differently?

Each person gets their own profile — vegetarian, allergies, dislikes, portion size. Forkcast looks for meals everyone can share first, then suggests easy swaps (like a second protein) when needed.

Is my data private?

Your meals, photos, and household details are yours. We don’t sell data to grocers or advertisers, and you can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.
