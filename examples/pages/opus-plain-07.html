    Questions

    Good
    questions.

    Do I have to follow the plan exactly?

    Not at all. Move dinners between days, skip a night for takeout, or swap anything you're not in the mood for. The list updates itself.

    Can I use my own recipes?

    Yes. Paste a link from almost any recipe site, or snap a photo of a cookbook page, and Mise will pull out the ingredients and steps.

    What about allergies and diets?

    Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, low-FODMAP and more. Allergies are hard filters, so those ingredients never appear in your plans.

    Does it work with grocery delivery?

    Household plans can send your list to Instacart, Walmart, Ocado and Tesco, with more stores coming. Everyone else can export to Notes, Reminders or plain text.

    What happens after the free trial?

    We'll remind you two days before it ends. If you don't upgrade, you'll drop to the free Taste plan and keep all your saved recipes.
