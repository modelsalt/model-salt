Questions we hear a lot

Usually asked while standing in front of an open fridge.

How long does planning actually take?

Most people finish their first plan in under five minutes. After that, each week takes about 90 seconds: review the draft, swap anything you're not feeling, and send the list to your phone.

What if my schedule changes mid-week?

Drag a meal to a different day, or tap "I'm out tonight" and Platefull reshuffles the rest of the week and adjusts your grocery list before you've bought anything extra.

Does it handle allergies and dietary preferences?

Yes. Set them once per household member — gluten-free, vegetarian, nut allergies, "no mushrooms ever" — and every plan respects them. You can also set a weekly budget target.

Can I use my own recipes?

Absolutely. Paste a link or type a family favorite, and it joins the rotation alongside ours. Many families run on a mix of both.

How much does it cost?

Your first two weeks are free, no card required. After that it's one simple monthly plan for the whole household — far less than one impulsive takeout order.
