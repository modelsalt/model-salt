A few good questions

Food for thought.

Getting into a new routine should feel simple.
Let’s start here.

Do I need to know my nutrition targets?

No. You can start with a simple preference, like adding more plants or making time for balanced meals. If you already have nutrition targets, use them to guide your choices.

Can I change the meals in my plan?

Absolutely. Swap meals to fit your tastes and schedule. A meal plan is there to give your week structure, with room to change your mind.

Will I need lots of time to cook?

Choose quick recipes for busy evenings and prep-ahead options for the week. Recipe times help you pick what fits before you get cooking.

Is Sprig a meal delivery service?

Sprig helps you plan what to cook and what to buy. You choose your groceries and prepare the meals yourself.
