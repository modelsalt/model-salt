FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to cook every single night?

Nope. Tell Ladle how many nights you want to cook and it fills the rest with leftovers, "assembly" meals, or leaves them open. Many users plan four nights and leave the weekend free.

What if I don't like a suggested meal?

Swipe it away. Ladle instantly offers alternatives that use similar ingredients so your grocery list barely changes — and it remembers your preference for next time.

Can I add my own family recipes?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Paste a URL, snap a photo of a recipe card, or type it in. Ladle extracts the ingredients and slots it into rotation alongside everything else.

Which grocery delivery services do you support?

Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Kroger, and Tesco at launch, with more coming. You can also export a plain checklist or share it to any messaging app.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel in two taps from your account page. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period and your recipes and preferences stay saved if you come back.
