A few things you might be wondering.

Do I have to plan every meal?

Start with dinner, or just the evenings that tend to get busy. A plan can include leftovers, eating out, and nights when you decide as you go.

What’s included in the sample plan?

Seven dinner ideas from the preview, suggested cooking times, and a short planning checklist. It’s a starting point for your week, rather than a full recipe collection.

Can I use the app here?

This page introduces Daywell with a sample of the weekly planning interface. The preview is static; you can download the sample plan to try the routine for yourself.
