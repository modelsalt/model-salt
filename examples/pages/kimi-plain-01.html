FAQ

Good questions

Do I have to follow the plan exactly?

Not at all. Every meal is swappable with one tap, and Mise instantly rebalances your grocery list. The plan is a starting point, not a contract.

Can it handle allergies and picky eaters?

Yes — that's where Mise shines. Set allergies as hard rules (they're never suggested), and mark disliked ingredients so plans work around them, per person.

How does the grocery list work?

Mise combines ingredients across your whole week, deduplicates them, scales quantities to your household size, and sorts everything by store aisle. Share it with family and check items off in real time.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes — three planned dinners a week, forever, no card required. Plus and Family come with a 14-day free trial, and you can cancel in two taps.

What platforms is Mise on?

iOS, Android, and the web — everything syncs instantly, so you can plan on your laptop and cook from your phone.
