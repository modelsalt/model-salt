FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at hello@sprig.app and a real person will reply within a day.

How does the "nutrition balance" actually work? +

Every recipe is tagged with its protein, fiber, vegetable servings and added sugar. When Sprig builds your week, it pairs breakfasts, lunches and dinners so each day meets the targets set from your goals. If you swap a meal, it adjusts the rest of that day to stay balanced.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial? +

No. You get all 14 days without entering any payment details. If you decide to stay, you choose a plan at the end. If not, nothing happens and you're never charged.

How much time will this take each week? +

Most people spend about 5 minutes approving their plan on Sunday and do one grocery run. Weeknight dinners take 30 minutes or less, and lunches are often planned leftovers that need no cooking at all.

Can I use it with dietary restrictions or allergies? +

Yes. Sprig supports vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free and high-protein plans, and you can exclude any ingredient you don't want. Sprig gives general guidance and isn't a replacement for medical advice.

Is Sprig a calorie-counting app? +

Not really. Calories are shown if you want them, but Sprig focuses on balance, meaning enough protein, fiber and vegetables, and less added sugar. You never have to log a meal by hand.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel. It takes two taps and there are no "are you sure?" mazes. Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days.
