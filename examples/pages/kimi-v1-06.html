Features

Everything dinner throws at you — handled.

One calm little app replacing the group chat polls, the recipe bookmarks graveyard, and the third grocery run of the week.

A fresh week, every week

Tell Plateful your tastes once. Every Sunday, a chef-crafted plan lands in your inbox — seasonal, varied, never boring.

Grocery lists that write themselves

Every ingredient summed, de-duplicated and sorted by aisle. Share it with the household or send it straight to pickup.

Pantry-first planning

Log what's on hand and Plateful plans around it first. Members toss roughly 40% less food within their first month.

Nutrition on autopilot

Calories, protein and macros per serving, balanced across your whole week — no spreadsheet, no mental math.

One-tap swaps

Not feeling Tuesday's salmon? Swap it in a tap — the grocery list and nutrition totals update themselves instantly.

Cook-along mode

Step-by-step recipes with built-in timers and big, flour-proof buttons your messy hands will appreciate.
