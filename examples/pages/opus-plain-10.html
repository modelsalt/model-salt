Questions

Good ones, too.

Something we missed? Write to hello@mise.app — a real person (usually mid-recipe) will answer.

Do I need to be a good cook?

Not at all. Every recipe is tagged by skill level and Mise starts you on easy wins. Cook mode walks you through each step with timers and photos.

How does the fridge scan work?

Take a photo (or a few) of your fridge and pantry. Mise identifies ingredients, estimates freshness, and you can correct anything with a tap. Photos are processed and then deleted — never stored or used for training.

Can I use my own recipes?

Yes. Paste a link, snap a photo of a cookbook page or a handwritten card, or type it in. Your recipes get mixed into plans alongside ours.

What if I don't like this week's plan?

Swap any meal with one tap, lock the ones you love, and regenerate the rest. Mise learns from every swap.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — from settings, in two taps, no phone call or guilt trip. Yearly plans are refunded pro-rata within the first 30 days.
