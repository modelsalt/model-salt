Questions

Good questions

Does it really take only five minutes?

Your first setup takes a couple of minutes while you tell us about your family. After that, most people plan a full week in three to five minutes, and it gets quicker as Simmer learns what you like.

Can I use my own recipes?

Absolutely. Paste a link from any recipe site, type one in, or snap a photo of a handwritten card. Your recipes get planned right alongside ours.

What if my kids are picky?

Give each person their own profile with likes, dislikes, and allergies. Simmer suggests meals the whole table will eat, and it can flag "build-your-own" nights when tastes are all over the place.

Can my partner and I share the grocery list?

Yes. On Family Table, everyone in your household sees the same plan and list in real time. Check off milk at the store and it disappears from their phone too.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your App Store or Google Play subscriptions with no phone calls or guilt trips. You'll keep the free plan and all your saved recipes.
