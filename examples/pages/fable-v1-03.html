FAQ

Questions people with restrictions actually ask

How do you make sure a recipe is actually safe for me?

Every ingredient in our library is mapped to its sub-ingredients and known allergen sources, and each recipe is screened against your full profile — including severity and trace-avoidance settings — before it appears. Each approved recipe shows a "why this is safe" summary. ClearPlate is a planning tool and does not replace advice from your allergist or doctor; always check product labels before eating.

Which allergens and diets do you support?

All 14 major regulated allergens (including the EU and FDA lists), plus 25+ less common ones such as sesame, mustard, lupin, sulphites and corn. Medical diets include coeliac/gluten-free, low-FODMAP, low-histamine, renal, diabetes-friendly, low-sodium and custom elimination protocols. You can also add free-text restrictions and we'll map them to ingredients.

Can I plan for a household where everyone has different restrictions?

Yes. The Household plan supports up to six profiles. For each meal you choose "one dish for all" (screened against every profile at once) or "parallel versions" that share prep but differ in a key ingredient.

What happens if my restrictions change?

Update your profile at any time. Your current week is re-screened immediately and any recipe that no longer passes is flagged with a checked replacement suggested.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. Sign up on the web, use ClearPlate for 14 days, and only add payment if you decide to continue. Nothing is charged automatically.

Are the safe-brand lists available in my country?

Safe-brand matching is currently available in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. Everywhere else you still get full recipe screening and generic grocery lists with allergen warnings.
