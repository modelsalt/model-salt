How it works

Three steps. Ten minutes on Sunday.

Set it up once, then let Sprout handle the weekly “what's for dinner?” for you.

1

Tell us about your table

How many you're feeding, who won't touch mushrooms, any allergies, and which nights are chaos (soccer, late meetings, dance class).

2

Get your week, instantly

Sprout drafts five dinners matched to your schedule — fast ones on busy nights, something cozier when you've got time. Swap any meal with a tap.

3

Shop once, cook calmly

One combined grocery list, sorted by aisle or sent straight to pickup. Each night, a timeline tells you exactly what to start and when.

FAQ

Questions parents actually ask.

What if my kids won't eat what Sprout suggests?

Tell us what each person likes and avoids. Sprout prioritizes meals everyone shares and suggests “deconstructed” versions (tacos, bowls, pizzas) where each kid builds their own. You can swap any meal in one tap, and Sprout learns from what you keep.

Do I have to cook every night?

Nope. Mark the nights you want off — takeout Friday, leftovers Thursday, dinner at Grandma's — and Sprout plans around them. You can plan anywhere from two to seven dinners a week.

Does it work with my grocery store?

Yes. Your list is sorted by aisle for in-store shopping, and you can export it to most major pickup and delivery services. Sprout never locks you into a specific retailer.

Can I add my own family recipes?

Absolutely. Paste a link or type a recipe in, and Sprout adds it to your rotation, builds the grocery items, and schedules it on nights that fit its cook time.

What happens after the free trial?

Your plan continues only if you choose one. We don't ask for a card up front, so there's nothing to forget to cancel. Your meal history stays saved for 90 days if you decide to come back.
