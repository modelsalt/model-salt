Questions, answered

How does Plateful choose my recipes?

During setup you'll tell us your dietary preferences, allergies, favorite cuisines, cooking skill, and time available. Our planner matches recipes to your profile, and learns from every swap, skip, and rating to get smarter over time.

Can I use my own recipes?

Yes! Import recipes from any website with one tap, or add family favorites manually. They'll appear in your rotation alongside Plateful suggestions and work with grocery lists and cook mode.

Does it work with grocery delivery?

Home Cook and Family Table plans can send your list directly to major grocery delivery services. You can also share a live list link with anyone — perfect when someone else is doing the shopping run.

What diets does Plateful support?

Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, keto, low-FODMAP, halal, and more — plus ingredient-level exclusions for allergies and picky eaters. Mix different profiles within one household.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts and no cancellation fees. If you cancel, you keep premium access until the end of your billing period, and your saved recipes stay yours forever on the free plan.
