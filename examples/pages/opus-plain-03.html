      Questions

      Good questions, answered.

      Do I have to follow the plan exactly?

      Not at all. Swap any meal, move nights around or mark a night as "eating out." Your grocery list updates instantly every time.

      Can it handle allergies and mixed diets?

      Yes. Set preferences per person, including vegetarian, gluten-free, nut allergies or "no cilantro, ever." Mise finds meals everyone can enjoy, or suggests easy tweaks for one plate.

      Where do the recipes come from?

      Our in-house test kitchen develops and tests every recipe for real weeknights. Most take under 35 minutes and use fewer than 10 ingredients. You can also import recipes from any website.

      Which grocery stores does delivery work with?

      At launch, Mise Plus sends your list to Instacart, Walmart, Kroger and Amazon Fresh in the US, with more on the way. You can always copy or print the list too.

      Can I cancel anytime?

      Yes, in two taps and with no phone calls. If you cancel Plus, you keep the free Pantry plan and all your saved recipes.
