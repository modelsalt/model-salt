A few things you might be wondering.

What does the grocery estimate include?

The demo adds the estimated ingredient cost of each selected recipe for your household size. It covers the dinners shown, rather than a full week of food. Actual checkout costs may be higher if you need to buy full packages of ingredients, oil, or spices.

What if my plan goes over budget?

Plenty shows the amount over your chosen budget immediately. Try removing a dinner or increasing the budget slider. For example, the three sample dinners cost an estimated $48 for two people; removing the tacos brings the plan to $30.

Can I try this without signing up?

Yes. This page is a working local demo: adjust your budget and household size, choose dinners, and download a grocery list. No account is created and your choices are not sent to a server. Refreshing the page resets the demo.
