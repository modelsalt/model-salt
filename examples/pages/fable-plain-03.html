FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@forkcast.app — a human replies, usually same day.

Does it handle allergies and diets?+

Yes. Set restrictions per person — vegetarian, gluten‑free, nut allergy, low‑FODMAP, halal, kosher, and more. Forkcast never suggests a recipe that conflicts, and it flags any recipe you import that does.

Can I use my own recipes?+

Absolutely. Paste a URL, take a photo of a cookbook page or handwritten card, or type it in. Forkcast parses ingredients and steps, lets you correct anything, then treats it like any other recipe for planning and lists.

What if I don't like a suggested meal?+

Swap it with one tap. You can ask for something specific ("faster," "vegetarian," "use what's in my fridge") or just shuffle. Your grocery list updates instantly.

Which grocery delivery services work with it?+

Plus and Household plans can send lists straight to Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart, and Kroger. Or just use the built‑in list in store — it's organized by aisle for whichever shop you pick.

Is there a family or shared option?+

The Household plan covers up to six people, each with their own preferences and dislikes. Everyone sees the same plan and list, and you can assign who's cooking which night.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes — no contracts, no hoops. Cancel in two taps from settings and keep access until the end of your billing period. Your recipes and plans export as a tidy PDF or spreadsheet whenever you want them.
