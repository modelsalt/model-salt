Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

Not seeing yours? Send it to hello@basketful.app.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan includes weekly meal plans, sale-matching for one store, and shopping lists. Basketful Plus ($4/month) adds multi-store price comparison and the receipt savings tracker. Most members say Plus pays for itself in the first week.

How do you know what's on sale near me?

We pull weekly circulars and digital coupons from more than 40 grocery chains. You choose your store when you sign up, and your plan refreshes every Wednesday when the new deals come out.

Can it handle picky eaters or allergies?

Yes. You can set dietary needs, allergies, and a "nope" list for each person. Sale-matching only suggests meals everyone at the table will eat.

Do I have to cook every night?

No. Pick how many nights you want to plan. A lot of members plan four dinners and leave the other nights for leftovers or a night out.
