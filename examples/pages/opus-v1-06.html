FAQ

Questions, answered (cheaply)

When does Pennypot launch?

We're aiming for January 2027, just in time for the post-Christmas money panic. Waitlisters get in up to two weeks early, in batches.

Is it really possible to eat well on £25 a week?

Yep. Our beta testers averaged £24.80 a week for 3 meals a day, all with actual vegetables. The trick is cooking smart, buying smart and making leftovers into new dishes.

I can barely boil an egg. Will this work for me?

Absolutely. Every recipe has a difficulty rating, step-by-step cooking mode and timers. Plenty of our recipes need nothing more than a microwave and some confidence.

Does it handle vegan, halal, gluten-free, or picky eating?

Yes to all of the above. You can also ban specific ingredients forever, so if mushrooms are your enemy, they'll never show up.

Which supermarkets do you support?

At launch: Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Aldi, Lidl and Morrisons, with more on the way. Prices update daily so your budget stays honest.

What happens to my email?

We'll use it to tell you when you're in, plus the odd recipe. That's it. We never sell your data, and you can unsubscribe in one click.
