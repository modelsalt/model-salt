Questions

Things people ask before they start

Do I have to cook every night? +

No. Tell Simmer how many nights you want to cook and it fills the rest with leftovers, planned takeout, or "fend for yourself" nights. Many users plan four dinners and let the week breathe.

What if I don't like a recipe it picks? +

Swipe it away and you'll get an alternative that still fits your week. Simmer also learns from this — reject mushrooms three times and they quietly stop showing up.

Can my whole household use one account? +

Yes. Household and Chef plans let everyone in your home see the plan, check off groceries, and mark what they ate. Each person can have their own dietary profile.

Does it work with my grocery store? +

Aisle sorting and price estimates cover most major US and UK chains. You can also export your list to Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, or just share it as plain text.

What happens when my trial ends? +

You drop to the free Starter plan automatically. No surprise charges, and you keep every recipe and list you've saved.
