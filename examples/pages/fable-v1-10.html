Features

Built for people who actually track.

Not another recipe feed. MacroMeal treats your targets as the starting point and works backwards to a plan you'll stick to.

Macro-first planning

Set protein, carbs, fat and calories per day — or per meal. Every plan lands within a few grams of target, automatically.

Cut, bulk or maintain

Pick a phase and a weekly rate. MacroMeal adjusts calories as your weight trends, so you're never guessing when to drop or add.

Swap without breaking the day

Out of salmon? Swap it for any food and the rest of your day rebalances to keep totals intact.

Grocery list that's already weighed

One tap turns the week into a shopping list in grams and servings, grouped by aisle, with meal-prep batches pre-calculated.

Training-day aware

Higher carbs on leg day, lower on rest days. Link your training split and your plan periodises itself.

Verified food data

Entries are cross-checked against USDA and manufacturer labels, so the numbers you log are the numbers you eat.

How it works

Three steps. Twelve minutes. Seven days sorted.

1

Set your targets

Enter your macros directly or let MacroMeal calculate them from bodyweight, activity and goal.

2

Pick the foods you like

Tick what you eat, cross out what you don't, add your go-to recipes. No tofu if you hate tofu.

3

Get your week

A full seven-day plan, grocery list and prep schedule. Swap anything, and the numbers stay on target.
