    Questions

    Good questions, short answers.

    Does it work for allergies and specific diets?

    Yes. Mise supports vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-FODMAP and halal plans, plus custom exclusions for any ingredient. Allergen filters are strict. A recipe with an excluded ingredient will never appear in your plan.

    Can I add my own recipes?

    Paste a link from almost any recipe site and Mise pulls in the ingredients and steps, without the life story. You can also type in Grandma's lasagna by hand. Imported recipes are part of Mise Plus.

    What if I only want to plan a few nights?

    Plan as many or as few as you like. Most people start with four cooked dinners and let Mise fill the rest with leftovers or "fridge-clearing" nights.

    How does pantry tracking work?

    Items you check off on the grocery list go into your pantry automatically. Mise subtracts what each recipe uses and nudges you when something is close to its use-by date. You can also add things by photo or barcode.
