FAQ

Good questions.

When does Sorted launch?

We’re opening to waitlist members in waves starting early 2027. The earlier you join, the earlier you get in.

How much will it cost?

Waitlist members get their first 3 months free. After that it’s planned at $5.99/month per household. You can cancel anytime.

Does it handle allergies and dietary preferences?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, low-FODMAP and more are supported, and you can set them per person within your household.

What if I don’t like a meal it picks?

Swap it with one tap. Sorted learns from every swap, so your plans keep getting closer to what you’d have chosen yourself.

Will it work with grocery delivery?

At launch you’ll be able to send your list to major delivery services in one tap. You can also just take the list to the store.
