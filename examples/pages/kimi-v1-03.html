How it works

Zero to planned
in 3 steps

No recipe hoarding, no spreadsheet gymnastics. Tell us about your table once, and Forkast does the weekly heavy lifting from then on.

Start planning free →
01

Tell us your tastes

A 90-second quiz covers diets, allergies, dislikes, cooking time, and how many mouths you're feeding. Forkast builds your flavor profile.

02

Get your weekly plan

Every Sunday, a fresh week of meals lands in your inbox — breakfast through dinner. Swap anything with one tap and the whole week rebalances.

03

Shop, cook, repeat

Your consolidated grocery list is ready when you are. Cook along with step-by-step mode, log what you loved, and next week gets even better.
